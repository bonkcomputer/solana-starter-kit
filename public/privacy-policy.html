Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 1, 2025
Effective Date: August 1, 2025

1. Introduction

BonkComputer Mobile ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Profile Information: Username, bio, profile picture (optional)
  • Wallet Information: Public wallet addresses (we never store private keys)
  • Communication: Messages you send through our support channels

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: App interactions, features used, session duration
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, app version
  • Performance Data: App crashes, errors, performance metrics

2.3 Blockchain Data

  • Transaction Data: Public blockchain transactions (inherently public)
  • Token Holdings: Token balances and transaction history from public blockchain

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain app functionality
  • Process transactions and display portfolio information
  • Calculate points and achievements
  • Enable social features and community interactions
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Provide customer support
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4.2 Service Providers

We may share information with trusted service providers who assist us in:

  • Cloud infrastructure and hosting
  • Analytics and performance monitoring
  • Customer support services

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and users' safety.

5. Third-Party Integrations

5.1 Solana Blockchain

Our app interacts with the Solana blockchain. All blockchain transactions are public and permanent.

5.2 Jupiter Exchange

Token swaps are processed through Jupiter Exchange. Please review their privacy policy.

5.3 Wallet Providers

Wallet connections are handled by third-party wallet providers. We do not store your private keys.

6. Data Security

  • We use industry-standard encryption for data transmission
  • We never store or transmit private keys
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Secure cloud infrastructure with access controls

7. Data Retention

  • Profile Data: Retained while your account is active
  • Usage Data: Retained for up to 2 years for analytics
  • Support Data: Retained for up to 3 years

8. Your Rights and Choices

8.1 Access and Control

  • View and update your profile information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

8.2 GDPR Rights (EU Users)

  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing

8.3 California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

  • Right to know what personal information we collect
  • Right to delete personal information
  • Right to opt-out of sale (we don't sell data)
  • Right to non-discrimination

9. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete the information immediately.

10. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international transfers.
